Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what information we collect when you use this website and how we use it.

Last updated: November 2026

Information we collect

When you place an order request, we collect the details you provide at checkout — your name, contact details and address — along with the products you have chosen.

How we use your information

We use this information only to review, confirm and fulfil your order request, and to support you with any questions about it.

Cookies and local storage

This website stores your cart in your browser’s local storage so that it is kept as you move between pages. This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only where necessary to fulfil your order, or where we are required to by law.

Data security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information you share with us from loss, misuse and unauthorised access.

Your rights

You may ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you. Please quote your order reference with any request.
