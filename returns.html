Returns & Refunds Policy

We want you to love everything you receive. If something isn’t right, this policy explains what you can expect.

Last updated: November 2026

Eligibility for returns

Unused ESSENCE products may be returned in their original condition and packaging. For hygiene and safety reasons, INDULGE food products and SAGE wellness products cannot be returned once opened.

Non-returnable items

Opened or used INDULGE and SAGE products, and any item that has been damaged through use or misuse.

How to request a return

Please quote your order reference when requesting a return. We’ll confirm whether your item is eligible and guide you through the next steps.

Refunds

Once a returned item has been received and inspected, approved refunds are issued to the original payment method.

Exchanges

Where possible, we’re happy to exchange an eligible ESSENCE item for another variant of the same product, or for a product of equal value.

Damaged or incorrect items

If your order arrives damaged or isn’t what you ordered, let us know with your order reference and a photograph, and we’ll make it right.
